# ÉDITION LIMITÉE · MAJORELLE

The entire Kiwi Systems website uses Majorelle, including existing logo marks and Systems / Commerce descriptors. The page background stays black.

- Nuit `#0A1230`: tinted glass and mobile navigation surfaces.
- Velours `#1E2F7C`: deep logo details.
- Vif `#3955D6`: primary logo color and sphere emission.
- Néon `#5BD5FF`: text accents, selected controls, highlights, focus rings, glows and secondary sphere light.

Original SVG geometry, white lettering, typography, copy, layout and all motion are preserved. The animated scene and matching fallback/social images already use Majorelle and remain unchanged. Kiwi OS and its master logos are untouched.

## Kiwi mark reference update

The Kiwi leaf mark uses the exact two dominant colors sampled from the user’s reference: dark blue `#222F77` and cobalt `#3F54CE`. White lettering, descriptors, dividers and the rest of the Majorelle website remain unchanged. This applies to the existing Systems, Commerce, Kiwi, standalone mark and favicon assets.

## Porcelain light edition

The page now uses porcelain `#F7F7F2`, black text, pale glass surfaces and cobalt status dots. Existing logos remain byte-for-byte unchanged, displayed on deep-blue backplates to preserve their white lettering. A final render-compositing pass places the original blue sphere glow onto porcelain; all sphere materials, lights, model, camera and motion remain unchanged. Fallback and social images are recaptured from this scene.

## Light-logo and sphere correction

Logo backplates are removed. Only the white iwi lettering becomes black; both blue K shapes, descriptors and dividers retain their colors. The sphere now uses 4-sample MSAA, alpha-to-coverage, anisotropic texture filtering, and a minimum 1.5× render scale (up to 2× on Retina). The light-background composite normalizes glow color before blending, avoiding the previous dark halo. Model and animation are unchanged.

## Cyan removed

Bright cyan is replaced throughout the active site with cobalt `#3F54CE`, including Systems / Commerce descriptors, decorative tints and the secondary sphere light. The two blue K colors, black lettering and porcelain background remain unchanged.
